What does the Admin Console do?

The ParkVision AI Admin Console is the central command center for parking lot operators, site engineers, and municipal facility controllers. Once logged in, you command three high-precision navigating headsets:

📐
Headset 1
Floor Plan Ingestion

Upload architectural layouts (AutoCAD/PNG/SVG), configure scale factors, and calibrate exact bay dimensions.

🎯
Headset 2
Coverage Mask (ROI)

Draw precise polygon boundary masks covering strictly the designated lot—not more and not less.

📹
Headset 3
Live Camera Output

Real-time multi-camera CCTV feeds updating dynamically every 10–30s based on vehicle traffic.

📋
